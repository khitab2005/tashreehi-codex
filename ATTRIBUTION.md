## Educational content

Original short Arabic and English summaries and quiz questions, written for Tashreehi with explicit references to OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e. No OpenStax illustrations or verbatim passages are redistributed. Each card links to a source. These summaries and translations have not undergone independent specialist review.

References: chapters 8.4, 11.6, 13.2, 19.1, 22.1, 23.6, 25.2, 25.3 and 27.2.

https://openstax.org/details/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e
